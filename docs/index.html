CopyMod

Clipboard history for the stock iOS keyboard

iOS 15.0 – 18.7 · rootless · arm64e

  • Records every text you copy, newest first.
  • Shows the history in a panel inside the keyboard.
  • Tap an entry to type it, long-press to copy, pin or delete.
  • Search field, light/dark mode, Arabic and English.
